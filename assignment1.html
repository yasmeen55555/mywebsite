My Company

Yasso Green Inc

A company focused on improving perfume bottles through sustainable design, efficient manufacturing, and environmentally friendly solutions.

My Project Title

Sustainable Refillable Perfume Bottle Design

My project is to improve traditional perfume bottles by making them refillable, reusable, and more durable. Since perfume bottles are made of glass and can easily break, my goal is to design a bottle that lasts longer and can be refilled instead of thrown away. This will reduce waste, save materials, and create a more sustainable product. Lean thinking will help reduce waste and unnecessary production steps, while Visual Factory Management will help organize the production process.

Computer Graphics in IE will be used to design and visualize my perfume bottle before making the physical product. I will use 2D drawings and 3D models to develop and improve the bottle's shape and design.

Yasso Green Inc. will collaborate with Verescence for the glass bottle, Aptar Beauty for the refill system and sprayer, Autodesk for the 2D and 3D design, and Berlin Packaging for sustainable packaging. Together, the five companies will create a refillable, reusable, and more durable perfume bottle while considering product safety, responsible manufacturing, and the environmental, economic, and social impacts of the product.
